What it does

From "saved" to "offer", with proof at every step

01

Track

A Kanban board for Saved, Applied, Interview, Offer and Rejected, with drag and drop.

02

Analyze

Paste a posting and get role, seniority, required skills, work mode and salary, validated against a schema.

03

Match

Every matched skill comes with a quote from your CV. Quotes that are not in it do not count, and the score is computed in code.

04

Write

A cover letter in the posting's language, checked by a reviewer that sends unsupported claims back for revision.

The AI crew

Four agents, one traced flight

The agents never talk to each other directly. An orchestrator passes typed, validated records between them, and every step is stored with its source, tokens and latency.

  1. 01 · Extractor

    Reads the posting into structured requirements.

  2. 02 · Matcher

    Searches your CV through tool calls and quotes its evidence.

  3. 03 · Writer

    Drafts the letter from verified facts only.

  4. 04 · Reviewer

    Approves it, or sends it back, at most twice.
